> This guide demonstrates how guides link to live database entries. On preview builds the linked figures are synthetic.

Copper Veins are the first mining nodes most characters meet. Open the [Copper Vein](/object/1731) page to see what was
observed from it, then compare [Copper Ore](/item/2770), [Rough Stone](/item/2835) and gem drops such as
[Shadowgem](/item/1210).

## Plan with the zone page

The [Tirisfal Glades](/zone/1420) page can be filtered to **Mining**. Its coordinate plots show where veins were observed,
aggregated to a coarse grid. Dense clusters are a good place to start a loop.

## Reading the gem numbers

Gems are rare, so their rates are often still marked *low sample*. Do not plan around a rare gem until the page shows a
moderate or high sample.
