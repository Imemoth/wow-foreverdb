ForeverDB records open-water fishing as one virtual source **per zone**, for example *Fishing - Tirisfal Glades*.
Fishing pools are recorded as their own sources, because pools have different catches.

## Comparing zones

Search the database with the acquisition filter set to **Fishing** to list every fishing zone with observations, then
open each zone's fishing page to compare catches.

## Pool positions are approximate

The game does not always expose a pool's exact position, so ForeverDB projects it from where you were fishing. Pool
locations are best-effort and are aggregated to a coarse grid.
