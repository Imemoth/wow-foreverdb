Companion **0.8.2-alpha** focuses on the Guildbook tab:

- larger roster rows and headers for big displays;
- summary cards for members, online, offline and the 30-minute refresh;
- visible/total counters that stay correct while filters are active;
- higher contrast between primary and secondary profession chips.

Guildbook information is private to the installation that collected it. It is **not** part of the public database and
is never published on this website.
